## The challenge

Using CO₂ as cushion gas in underground natural-gas storage requires an assessment of investment costs, operating conditions and market uncertainty.

## Contribution

The study evaluated Polish storage options through site selection, CAPEX and OPEX modeling, and sensitivity analysis.

## Engineering approach

Costs covered drilling, injection, gas processing and decommissioning. A Monte Carlo analysis with 10,000 iterations varied gas prices, electricity costs and technical parameters, alongside seasonal price differences and potential CO₂ storage benefits.

## Results

Over 90% of the simulated scenarios produced a positive net present value under the study assumptions. High electricity prices and small seasonal gas-price differences were identified as important risks.
