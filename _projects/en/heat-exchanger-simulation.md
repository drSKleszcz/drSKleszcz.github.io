## The challenge

Experiments alone cannot capture every process inside a periodically reversing counterflow heat exchanger, particularly the formation and evaporation of water films.

## Contribution

A three-dimensional transient model was developed to investigate heat and mass transfer and support the microclimate control system design.

## Engineering approach

The model coupled continuity, momentum and energy equations with Eulerian Wall Film and species transport models. Periodic boundaries reduced the domain to two representative channels; prototype tests supported validation.

## Results

The analysis identified how velocity, temperature, humidity and cycle switching time affect performance. Simulation data supported development of the device control algorithm.
