## The challenge

Presenting specialist calculation software requires clear explanations of its capabilities, calculation methods and outputs.

## Contribution

Designed and developed the Orifice product website, combining product information, technical explanations and software screenshots.

## Approach

Organized the website around software information, reports, a demo, pricing and contact, with a dedicated explanation of the calculation method.

## Results

The published website provides a public reference for understanding the software and exploring its interface, calculation approach and outputs.
