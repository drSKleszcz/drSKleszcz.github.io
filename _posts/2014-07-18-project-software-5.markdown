  <div style="text-align: justify;">
    <p><strong>OriCalc</strong> is a Windows desktop application for designing orifice flow meters according to <strong>ISO 5167:2003</strong>. It is now a commercial product with its own website, <a href="https://orifice-software.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">orifice-software.com</a>, offering a free demo, a lifetime licence and enterprise customisation.</p>

    <h5>Calculations</h5>
    <ul>
        <li><strong>Orifice sizing:</strong> the orifice diameter for a given maximum flow rate and differential pressure.</li>
        <li><strong>Differential pressure:</strong> the pressure drop for a known orifice diameter and flow rate.</li>
        <li><strong>Flow rate:</strong> the actual flow from the orifice diameter and the measured pressure drop.</li>
        <li><strong>Scope:</strong> corner, flange and D and D/2 pressure tappings; pipe diameters from 50 mm to 1000 mm; Reynolds number above 5000.</li>
    </ul>

    <h5>Engineering detail</h5>
    <ul>
        <li>Discharge coefficient calculated over the full measurement range, plus a simplified flow equation for differential pressure transmitters.</li>
        <li>Manufacturing dimensions of the plate, pressure tap distances and allowed pipe roughness.</li>
        <li>Minimum straight pipe lengths upstream and downstream, with the additional measurement error when they are shorter.</li>
        <li>Real gas properties of humid air and other fluids, with uncertainty analysis of the whole measurement chain.</li>
    </ul>

    <h5>Interface and reports</h5>
    <ul>
        <li>Technical diagram of the selected orifice configuration and all fluid parameters on one screen.</li>
        <li>Two interactive charts of flow measurement error across the operating range.</li>
        <li>One-click engineering report with sizing, fluid parameters, error charts and a technical drawing with pressure tappings.</li>
    </ul>

    <h5>Product and website</h5>
    <ul>
        <li>Redesigned website with a product tour, calculation method, pricing and demo request.</li>
        <li>Online payments, licence generation and device-bound activation of the desktop application.</li>
        <li>Developed together with Ali Karimi, PhD.</li>
    </ul>
